# Runway model

Last updated 2026-05-31 by Dana Okafor.

- Cash on hand 2026-05-31: **$3,069,000** (Mercury).
- Average net burn, Jan to May: **$130,000** per month. Plan for the rest of the year: **$140,000** per month.
- **Runway: about 22 months at $140k per month**, which takes us to roughly March 2028.

Assumptions:
- Six H2 hires are budgeted inside the $140k average, because they start late in the year.
- MRR keeps growing ~10% a month, so net burn stays flat even as headcount grows.
