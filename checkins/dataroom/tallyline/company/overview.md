# Tallyline: company overview

Updated 2026-07-01 by Dana Okafor.

- **What we do:** AI bookkeeping for independent restaurants. We pull sales from the restaurant's POS (Toast, Square, Clover), match them to bank deposits and supplier invoices, and close the books every week instead of every quarter.
- **Founded:** March 2024, New York.
- **Founders:** Dana Okafor (CEO, ex-controller at a 40-location restaurant group), Priya Raman (CTO, ex-Square payments infrastructure).
- **Pricing:** $199 to $349 per location per month.
- **Funding:** $600k pre-seed (Aug 2024, angels). $3.2M seed closed 2026-02-12, led by Northbeam Ventures ($1.5M), with Ridgeline Capital ($1.0M) and angels.
- **Customers:** 289 restaurants (2026-06-30), mostly in NYC, NJ and Philadelphia.
