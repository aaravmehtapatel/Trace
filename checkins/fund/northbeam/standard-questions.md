# Northbeam quarterly check-in: standard questions

Asked of every portfolio company each quarter. The VC agent adds 2 to 4 questions tailored to each company's last update.

1. MRR at the end of each month of the quarter.
2. Net burn per month for the quarter, and cash at quarter end.
3. Runway in months at the current burn, and the assumptions behind it.
4. Monthly revenue or logo churn for the quarter.
5. Headcount at quarter end, with hires and departures.
6. Progress against the plan's quarterly targets.
7. Top risks right now.
8. Asks for the fund.
